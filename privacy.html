Privacy policy

What happens with your data
on this site.

data/diary is a small personal blog. It doesn't sell data, run ads, or track you across other sites. Here's exactly what's collected and why.

Analytics

This site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to see roughly how many people visit and which posts get read. Umami doesn't use cookies and doesn't build a profile tied to you individually — it reports aggregate numbers (page views, referrers, rough location from IP), not who you are.

Comments

If a post has comments enabled, they're powered by Cusdis, a lightweight third-party comment widget. When you submit a comment, Cusdis stores the name you enter, the comment text, and your IP address (used to filter spam). No account or login is required — just a name.

Comments are held for moderation before they appear. If you'd like a comment removed or edited after posting, reach out and it'll be handled.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask what data is held about you, request it be corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. For anything related to comments you've submitted, get in touch and it'll be sorted out directly. For Cusdis specifically, you can also reach out to them via their own privacy policy.
