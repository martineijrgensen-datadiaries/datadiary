Privacy policy

What happens with your data
on this site.

data/diary is a small personal blog. It doesn't sell data, run ads, or track you across other sites. Here's exactly what's collected and why.

Analytics

This site uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool to see roughly the number of visits. Umami doesn't use cookies.

Comments

If a post has comments enabled, they're powered by Cusdis, a lightweight third-party comment widget. When you submit a comment, Cusdis stores the name you enter and the comment text. No account or login is required — just a name.

Comments are held for moderation before they appear. If you'd like a comment removed or edited after posting, reach out and it'll be handled.
